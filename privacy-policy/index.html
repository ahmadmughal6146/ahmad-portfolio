Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026 • Effective Immediately

1. Introduction & Overview

Welcome to the official portfolio of Muhammad Ahmad (Ahmad Graphixs GFX) ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any personal information you share with us through this website (ahmad-portfolio-wine-omega.vercel.app / ahmadgraphixsgfx.netlify.app).

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect your personal data when you browse our portfolio, view projects, or submit an inquiry.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information that you voluntarily provide to us, as well as basic technical data required to operate the site:

  • Contact & Inquiry Details: When you submit a message through our inquiry form, you provide your name, email address, selected service category, and project message.
  • Communication Data: If you reach out to us directly via WhatsApp or email, we may retain your message history to communicate regarding your design or development projects.
  • Preferences & Local Storage: We use your browser's local storage solely to remember your chosen visual theme (Dark Obsidian, Luxe Gold, or Cyberpunk Neon). No tracking cookies are used to identify you across third-party websites.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly for legitimate business and client communication purposes:

  • To respond to your design and development inquiries, discuss project briefs, and provide custom quotes.
  • To provide ongoing graphic design, branding, and frontend engineering services as requested.
  • To maintain, optimize, and ensure the security and speed of this portfolio web application.
  • We never sell, rent, trade, or share your personal information with third-party marketers or advertisers.

4. Third-Party Services & External Links

Our website connects with select third-party platforms to enhance communication and showcase verified design portfolios:

  • WhatsApp (Meta): Clicking our WhatsApp direct chat button redirects you to WhatsApp's secure messaging platform under Meta's Privacy Policy.
  • Professional Social Networks: Links to our verified profiles on LinkedIn, Behance, Instagram, and GitHub.
  • Google Fonts: Web fonts are loaded securely via Google Fonts CDN without tracking personal user identities.

5. Data Security & Storage

We employ industry-standard technical measures, including SSL encryption (HTTPS), clean code standards, and secure server hosting (Vercel & Netlify), to protect your personal data from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

6. Your Rights & Choices

You have full control over your personal data. At any time, you may:

  • Request a copy of any personal information we hold about you.
  • Request the correction, update, or complete deletion of your contact records.
  • Clear your browser's local storage and cache to reset visual theme preferences.
